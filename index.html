Northline delivers commercial, residential, industrial and civil works, from focused fit-outs to complex multi-storey developments.

When should a contractor join the project?+

Early involvement usually creates the greatest value. It lets the delivery team test cost, buildability, sequencing and procurement decisions while changes are still inexpensive.
