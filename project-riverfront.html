Riverfront Workplace

Westlands, Nairobi · 2025 · 12,400 m²

Client
Riverfront Developments

Scope
Eight-storey commercial workplace

Services
General contracting and fit-out

A refined city workplace built around flexible floorplates, natural light and efficient long-term operation.

The challenge

A constrained urban site required precise delivery sequencing, limited material storage and continuous coordination with neighbouring properties.

The response

Northline used phased procurement, just-in-time deliveries and weekly digital coordination reviews to keep structural, services and façade packages aligned.

The result

The project reached practical completion in 2025 and now provides efficient, adaptable office space for a growing mix of Nairobi businesses.
