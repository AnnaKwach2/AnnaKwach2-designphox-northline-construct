About Northline

Good construction is an act of alignment.

We align people, information and decisions so the work in the field is safer, smoother and more predictable.

Our perspective

Northline Construct is a Nairobi-based building and civil contractor delivering commercial, residential and industrial projects across Kenya.

Our model is built around direct senior involvement, clear accountability and practical problem solving. We believe clients should understand where their project stands, what comes next and where attention is needed.
